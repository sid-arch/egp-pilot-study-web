CHAPTER 01 / ORIGIN

Numbers are abstract.
Stories aren't.

Euler's Golden Pie is a memory-based system built around transforming numerical information into structured visual stories that can be reconstructed and recalled.

In 2026, the project crossed a line: from something designed and developed into something placed in front of real participants.

WHY RUN A PILOT?

Because an idea changes
