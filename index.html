01 / THE IDEA

Numbers are abstract.
Stories aren’t.

Euler's Golden Pie is a memory-based system built around transforming numerical information into structured visual stories that can be reconstructed and recalled.

In 2026, the project crossed a line: from something designed and developed into something placed in front of real participants.

03 / WHY WE DID IT

Because an idea changes
when people touch it.

The pilot was designed to observe how participants learned and used EGP in practice, examine their experience across repeated sessions, and identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for refinement.

Ten participants took the system beyond the drawing board and into a real pilot environment.
