01 / THE IDEA

Numbers become
stories.

Euler's Golden Pie is a memory-based system built around transforming numerical information into structured visual stories that can be reconstructed and recalled.

In 2026, EGP moved from development into a real pilot environment. Ten participants signed up, trained across seven days, completed assessments before and after the study, and put their learning into action in a final 50-digit challenge.

03 / WHY WE DID IT

A system isn't truly tested
until people use it.

The pilot was designed to observe how participants learned and used EGP in practice, examine their experience across repeated training sessions, and identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for refinement.

Pre- and post-assessments created a structured before-and-after view of participant performance. The study was exploratory; its results should be interpreted as pilot evidence rather than proof of causation.
