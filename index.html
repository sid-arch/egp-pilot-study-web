01 · THE PROJECT

What is Euler's
Golden Pie?

Euler's Golden Pie (EGP) is a memory-based system built around transforming numerical information into structured visual stories that can be reconstructed and recalled.

The 2026 pilot study was created to take EGP beyond development and place it in front of real participants. Ten participants signed up and took part in the study, completing guided sessions and putting in strong effort throughout the program.

Participants completed a pre-test before training and a post-test after the study, allowing the project team to compare performance before and after the pilot period.

03 · WHY THE PILOT

Why We Conducted
the Pilot

The pilot was designed to evaluate how participants learned and used Euler's Golden Pie in practice, to observe their experience across multiple training sessions, and to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for refinement.

It also created a structured setting for collecting pre- and post-assessment data and for observing participant performance during a final recall competition.
